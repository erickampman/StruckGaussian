## [0.9.0] - 2026-11-14

First public preview.

### Added

- StruckSynth with its object's modes drawn as a Gaussian cloud around the note: one Width knob from
  a chorus on one pitch to a wide clang.
- A seed makes a preset's cloud repeatable, or a new cloud on every note.
- MIDI 2.0 notes taken directly, so a Pitch 7.9 note plays its exact pitch; MPE and CLAP note
  expressions too.
- CLAP plugin for Linux (x86-64) and macOS 26 or later (Apple Silicon).
